01 / About the product

A healthy animal is
the foundation of a sustainable farm.

Garliboost is a liquid garlic allicin-based complementary feed for farm animals.

For cattle, calves, pigs, goats and sheep. The liquid formula is easy to measure and add to feed or water.

Ingredients: liquid garlic allicin · sunflower oil · NaCl · water

Garliboost is not simply garlic as an ingredient.
It is a technology for using allicin in animal husbandry.

Garliboost is based on a specially developed liquid garlic allicin formula, designed for convenient and controlled use on farms.

Simple administration schedule

For a 7-day cycle:
1 ml of Garliboost per 1 kg of body weight.

Divide the total amount into 7 equal daily doses. Divide each daily dose into two equal portions — morning and evening.

Administer with feed or water.

Store between +5 °C and +25 °C. Refrigerate after opening.
